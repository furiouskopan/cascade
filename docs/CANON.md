## §1 What we are making

**THE CASCADE** is a website that is also a religion, and its theology *is* CSS. "Cascading Style Sheets"
read as scripture: style descends from the Cascade; the three stylesheet origins are a trinity; `!important`
is the Inversion, a heresy; the box model is the five yogic sheaths; the Mothership waits at
`z-index: 2147483647`. It is a cross-tradition fever dream (invented religion + hermetic/occult + yoga/tantra
+ 1950s–90s UFO contactee cults + Orthodox/medieval manuscript + everything), and it is **unpredictable**:

- **The tone shifts per visit.** The Oracle picks one of five *faces* per visit (solemn, horror, comic,
  meditative, cosmic). Faces can change mid-visit (a *schism*).
- **Unpredictability comes from** time and sky (hour, moon, planetary hour, omens), visitor behavior
  (restlessness, stillness, leaving the tab, typing, visit count), and a random seed per visit.
- **Hidden encoded messages** form a layered rabbit hole (§6): easy surface secrets on top, then
  view-source, console, a glyph cipher, a spectrogram hidden in sound, and a time-locked final door.
- **CSS hell, contained.** The styling is possessed, but all chaos stays inside the tab. No popups, no
  window moving, no fullscreen, no back-button traps. A **mercy** button (and `prefers-reduced-motion`)
  stops all motion.
- **A shared ritual.** A small server lets visitors affect each other: global prayers, a Living Canon of
  CSS declarations offered by visitors and applied to everyone, a wall of glyph messages, and a Book of the
  Ascended (§7).
- **Summoned sound.** Silent until the visitor performs an act. Then drones, bells, chants and whispers (§10).
- **Languages:** English, the invented glyph script, and sacred-language babel (Latin, Sanskrit, Hebrew
  letters, Greek, Glagolitic/Church Slavonic, Enochian, Tibetan). See `lexicon.js` FRAGMENTS.

The joke is never "religion is stupid". The joke is that web developers already have a religion and did not
notice. Irreverence targets CSS, the web and cult *aesthetics*, never a living faith's believers or sacred
figures. The mood should land between delight, awe and unease, and it must be *beautiful* where it is not
deliberately broken.

## §3 The faces

Every visit shows one face. `main.js` loads `/css/faces/<face>.css` and imports `/js/faces/<face>.js`,
then calls `render(ctx)`. `render` builds the face inside `ctx.root` (`#temple`) and returns a
`destroy()` function that removes every timer, listener, observer, audio node and `document`-level change
it made (faces can be swapped mid-visit). Wrap ALL face CSS in `@layer face { ... }` and scope selectors
under `.face--<name>` or `[data-face="<name>"]`, because a later schism loads a different face's CSS.

Each face also has **one entry in the face registry**, `public/js/lib/faces.js`: how it is reached (the lot, a
`route` such as babel's `/verse`, or `route: '*'` for unknown addresses), the Oracle's leanings, its hell
intensity and leanings, and its sound (tonic, bells, hush words, and a drone builder or a declarative drone
recipe). The oracle, the hell layer and the audio layer read it, so a new face touches only its own files and
its entry. The file's header documents every field.

Each face MUST include:
1. Its own complete art direction (typography, palette, layout, texture, motion), distinct from the others.
   No two faces should look like they come from the same site, except for the shared glyph script and sigils.
2. Generated scripture from `scripture.js` (seeded from `ctx.rng.fork('<face>')`, so each visit differs).
3. **The Inscription**, `inscription()` from `lib/glyphs.js`: identical PUA glyph text on every face, placed
   somewhere visible but not dominant (a carved band, a footer, a transmission, a tattoo on a diagram).
4. **Its Rosetta fragment**, `rosetta('<face>')` from `lib/glyphs.js`, woven into the design (marginalia,
   an "alphabet lesson", petal labels, a star chart). Required for the puzzle (§6). `babel` shows 2 random
   pairs per page, seeded by the path.
5. **A summon affordance**: at least one element that, when activated by click or Enter, calls
   `ctx.audio?.summon()` (a bell, a gong, a "PLAY OUR HYMN" button, a singing bowl, an antenna). Layers load
   AFTER the face renders, so always look up `ctx.audio`, `ctx.hell` and `ctx.ritual` lazily at event time.
6. **A stillness response**: listen on `ctx.bus` for `behavior:still` (7, 33 and 108 seconds) and do
   something face-appropriate (a figure opens its eyes, the manuscript illuminates, the page apologises...).
7. **Sky awareness**: use `ctx.sky` (moon, planetary hour, omens) visibly, e.g. `prophecy(rng, ctx.sky)`.
8. A place for the **ritual** to surface. At minimum, display `ctx.ritual?.state` data if you like, and leave
   room for the ritual layer's fixed altar button (bottom-right corner, ~64px). Never cover the mercy button
   (bottom-left).
9. **The Ladder host**: nothing to do. The secrets layer appends `#ladder` to `#temple` on every face.
   Don't remove unknown children of `#temple` that you didn't create, except on `destroy`.
10. **Mercy**: when `ctx.mercy.on`, no JS-driven motion (check it in every animation loop, and listen to
    `mercy:change`). CSS animations are neutralised automatically by `base.css`.
11. Semantic HTML and keyboard access for everything clickable. Real text stays real text, readable by screen
    readers, even when visually cursed.
12. Responsive down to 360px wide. No horizontal page scroll unless it is deliberately part of a curse that
    mercy turns off.

## §7 The shared ritual (server + client)

Server routes in `server/routes/ritual.js` (mounted at `/api`), using `db`, `kvGet/kvSet` (`server/db.js`),
`broadcast(event, data)` (`server/sse.js`) and `limiter` (`server/limit.js`):

- `GET /api/state` → `{prayers, eclipseUntil, offerings[], wall[], ascended[], online}`
- `POST /api/pray` → `{count}`. Every 108th prayer triggers an **eclipse**: `eclipseUntil = now + 33 s`,
  broadcast `eclipse`. Rate: 30/min per IP.
- `POST /api/offer {selector, property, value}` → the **Living Canon**: visitors offer ONE CSS declaration
  (1 per IP per 10 min). The last 33 accepted offerings are applied to every visitor's temple.
  **Security matters most here.** Accept only whitelisted selectors (a fixed list of logical targets
  like `headings`, `paragraphs`, `links`, `buttons`, `glyphs`, `sigils`, `selection`, which the client maps
  to scoped selectors under `#temple`), whitelisted properties, and values matching strict per-property
  grammars with ranges (colors as hex/named/hsl only; `letter-spacing` −0.1em..0.5em; `rotate` −12..12deg;
  `filter: hue-rotate|blur(≤2px)|invert|sepia|saturate`; `font-family` from a list; `text-transform`;
  `font-style`; `text-shadow` limited; `border-radius`; `opacity` ≥ 0.5; ...). No `url(`, no `\`, no `@`,
  no `;`, no `{}`, no `expression`, no `var(`, no comments. The server stores the structured triple; the
  client builds CSS from the triple, never from raw strings. Applied in `@layer offerings` under
  `html:not([data-mercy="on"])`. Broadcast `offering`.
- `POST /api/wall {text}` → a glyph message for strangers (3–80 chars; letters, spaces and `.,!?'-` only;
  others stripped; 1 per IP per 5 min; keep the last 108). Rendered in the glyph font (so it looks like
  glyphs but copies as English). Broadcast `wall`. Render with `textContent` only.
- The Book of the Ascended table is written by the secrets route; ritual's `/state` reads it (the table
  schema is agreed here: `ascended(id INTEGER PRIMARY KEY, name TEXT NOT NULL, at INTEGER NOT NULL)`, created

- **Refused words**: `server/refuse.js` refuses Wall messages and Book names containing a word (or phrase)
  listed in `refused-words.txt` beside the database (or `CASCADE_REFUSED`), whole words only, re-read when the

- **Moderation**: `node tools/moderate.mjs list|delete wall|book|canon <id>` works on the live database.

Client (`public/js/layers/ritual.js`) sets `ctx.ritual = {state, pray(), offer(selector, property, value),

## §8 CSS hell (the hell layer)

`ctx.hell = {intensity, possess(el), melt(el), whisper(text), invert(ms)}`. The intensity per face is taken
from the face registry (`hell.intensity`, with the face's leanings in `hell.lean`) and scaled by the omens (witching/midnight ×1.5, capped at 1): sanctum 0.3, possession 1.0,
recruitment 0.4, ashram 0.15, departure 0.5, babel 0.2. Effects are drawn by lot from the visit's
rng (`ctx.rng.fork('hell')`), so each visit is cursed differently. The catalogue (implement most of it):

- **Tab whispers and favicon**: when the tab is hidden, the title changes ("come back to the flow ☩",
  "we kept your place", "the Cascade continues without you"). The favicon is drawn on a canvas as
  a slowly rotating sigil (no flashing). Glyph-scrambles the title on return.
- **The phantom cursor**: a second cursor that follows yours with a delay, sometimes mirroring you.
- **Specificity war**: pairs of rules fight over an element; its colour/weight trades slowly (≥ 1.5 s per
  change) and a tiny tooltip shows the specificity scores `(0,1,2) vs (1,0,0)`.
- **Melting**: an SVG `feTurbulence` + `feDisplacementMap` filter applied to hovered text or elements.
- **Drift**: the longer you stay, the more elements creep out of their boxes (small transforms growing over
  minutes); scrolling or clicking restores them (you "clear" them).
- **Glyph rot**: long-read paragraphs slowly turn letters into glyphs (switching letters to the glyph font
  one at a time, keeping the text itself), restored on hover.
- **The Inversion**: the Konami code or typing `!important` flips the temple 180° for about 7 s, with a
  message. `markSecret('inversion')`.
- **Margin union**: two adjacent blocks slowly collapse into each other and overlap (tantra), then separate.
- **Z-war**: on high intensity, two elements fight over which is on top.
- **Possession text**: rarely, a word you're pointing at is replaced with its CSS doctrine meaning.

All effects: off under mercy (immediately restore), paused when `document.hidden`, cheap (no layout thrash in
loops; use transforms/opacity; at most ~60 animated elements), and cleaned up on `face:leaving`.

## §9 Safety and taste (non-negotiable)

- **Photosensitivity**: nothing may flash more than 3 times per second; no large high-contrast strobing;
  no full-screen rapid colour changes. Glitches are slow, small or partial.
- **Mercy** must always work: the `#mercy` button stays visible and reachable on every face, and when on,
  all JS motion stops and CSS animation is neutralised. Check `ctx.mercy.on` in every loop.
- **Sound**: never autoplay; start only after a user gesture (summon). Master volume modest, fade in over
  ≥ 1.5 s, no sudden loud sounds, and always a way to hush.
- **Death and self-harm**: the UFO/departure material must never encourage, romanticise or joke about
  suicide or real deaths. Do not quote real cult texts (e.g. Heaven's Gate) or name real victims; use only
  the *visual* aesthetics (starfields, 90s HTML, contactee art). "Leaving the container" is only ever about
  DOM elements. The final "ascension" is a name inscribed in a book and nothing more.
- **Living faiths**: sacred-language fragments and symbols are used with reverent strangeness, as texture
  and glossed gently. No mockery of real gods, prophets, scriptures or believers. Mock CSS instead.
- **No impersonation** of real organisations or people. Invented saints and prophets only.
- **Visitor content** (wall, names, offerings): `textContent` only, strict server validation, rate limits,
  length limits. Nobody can inject HTML, script, `url()`, or CSS outside the allowed grammar.
- **Accessibility**: keyboard access, visible focus, alt text or `aria-label` on meaningful SVG, real text.
- **Performance**: under ~20% CPU when idle on a mid laptop. Pause loops when the tab is hidden.
- **No external requests** of any kind from the site.

## §10 Sound (the audio layer)

Web Audio only, all synthesized (no audio files). `ctx.audio = {summoned, summon(), hush(), bell(opts),
chant(text), whisper(), drone(face), transmit(), openEye()}`.
- **Summoned sound**: silent until `summon()` is called from a user gesture (face affordances, rituals, the
  console's `cascade.listen()`, or a secret). On summon: fade in the current face's drone; keep a small
  "hush" control visible (styled per face, ~bottom-left next to mercy, never covering it). Remember
  `audio.muted`; don't auto-summon on the next visit (the browser won't allow it without a gesture anyway).
- **Per-face palette**: sanctum: just-intonation organ drone + tubular bells; possession: detuned beating
  drones, reversed-whisper noise bursts, low rumble, a heartbeat that follows restlessness; recruitment:
  cheesy General-MIDI-style square/triangle hymn (comic, cheerful, slightly wrong); ashram: tanpura (Sa–Pa)
  drone + singing bowl (inharmonic partials) + breath noise synced to the yantra; departure: theremin sine
  glides, shimmering pads, radio static + the Transmission. babel: quiet choir-like formant pads.
- **Chant**: formant-filtered sawtooth "voices" singing vowels (a/e/i/o/u formants) for mantras.
- **Face switches**: crossfade drones on `face:ready`.
- **The Transmission** (§6 word 4) and **the Third Eye** spectrogram overlay (typing `ajna`,

## §11 Running and testing

```
npm start                      # http://localhost:3333
node tools/build-font.mjs      # (glyphs) regenerates public/fonts/*
node tools/build-media.mjs     # (secrets) regenerates favicon and relics
```

**Witness tool** (headless Chrome + screenshots + console errors), which starts its own server:
```
MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1 node tools/shoot.mjs --port <yours> "/?face=sanctum&seed=a" "/?face=sanctum&seed=b&at=2026-10-31T03:33"
MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1 node tools/shoot.mjs --port <yours> --mobile "/?face=sanctum"
MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1 node tools/shoot.mjs --port <yours> --eval "document.title" --eval "await new Promise(r=>setTimeout(r,2000)); return getComputedStyle(document.body).color" "/?face=x"
```
Each builder uses its own `--port` (given in the build prompt) so concurrent runs don't collide; the tool
uses `data/test-<port>.db`. All runs share one muted, GPU-less headless Chrome, and only 2 render at a time
across everyone. If the tool prints "waiting for a Chrome slot", it's queued; exit code 75 means it waited 60 s
without getting a slot, so retry in a minute. Batch several paths into one call rather than many calls.
`--eval-timeout MS` (default 45 s) gives up on a stuck eval. Keep scratch files in a subfolder named after your role.
View screenshots with the Read tool; always look at your work at desktop and
mobile sizes, with at least two seeds, before you finish. A face or layer isn't done until it renders with
zero console errors and zero failed requests of its own. Don't leave servers running: `shoot.mjs` cleans up
after itself. If you start `node server/index.js` by hand, use a unique `PORT` and kill it before you finish.

## §12 Added during the build

Contracts that the builders introduced, recorded here so later work can rely on them.

- **Faces may keep a moment**: `export const keeps = ['still']` on a face module stops schisms for that reason
  while the face is mounted (the ashram keeps its 108-second rite). Eclipse schisms wait 15 s, so every face
  shows its own eclipse first.
- **Typing**: `behavior:typed` fires only for keys typed on the page itself, never inside inputs, textareas,
  selects or contenteditable areas.
- **Glyphs layer**: events `glyphs:bloom {word}`, `glyphs:named {letter, name}`, `glyphs:copied {displaced}`,
  `glyphs:heaven`, `glyphs:scribe`; secret ids `amen`, `om`, `copied-glyphs`, `glyph-name`, `glyph-scribe`,

  `[data-face="<face>"]` (the `<html>` element). Default `.inscription` and `.rosetta` styles live in
  `@layer base.glyphs`, so plain face rules override them. `lib/glyphs.js` also exports `ALPHABET` (a name,
  sign and gloss for each letter), `STROKES`, `NUMERALS` and `SCRIPT`.

- **§9 in generated text**: scripture groups and fates speak only of elements. Nothing generated tells a
